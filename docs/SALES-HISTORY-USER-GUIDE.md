# 📖 Sales History: How to View Product Details

**Quick User Guide** - Updated October 7, 2025

---

## 🎯 What's New?

You can now **click on any sale** in the Sales History to see exactly what products were sold!

---

## 📝 Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Go to Sales History

1. Click **"Sales"** in the left sidebar
2. You'll see the **Sales History** tab (should be selected by default)

---

### Step 2: Find a Sale

Look at the sales table. You'll see:

| Receipt # | Date | Customer | Items | Amount | Status | Payment |
|-----------|------|----------|-------|--------|--------|---------|
| **►** REC-202510-01220 | Oct 3 | TechPro | **► 1 items** | GH¢3,166 | ✅ COMPLETED | CASH |

**Notice the ► arrow** next to the Items count - this means you can expand it!

---

### Step 3: Click to Expand

**Click anywhere on the row** to see product details:

**The row will expand** and show:

### Step 4: Click Again to Collapse

**Click the row again** to hide the details and collapse it back to one line.

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

### ✅ What You Can Do:

- **Click any sale** to see what was sold
- **View all products** in that sale
- **Check prices** and discounts
- **See who processed** the sale
- **Verify payment method**
- **Only one sale expands** at a time (keeps it clean!)

### 📊 What You'll See:

For each product sold, you'll see:
- **Product name** (e.g., "MS Office Home & Business")
- **SKU code** (e.g., "SOFT-DL-0002")
- **Category** (e.g., "Software")
- **Quantity sold** (e.g., "13.00")
- **Unit price** (e.g., "GH¢243.56")
- **Discount** (if any was applied)
- **Line total** (Qty × Price - Discount)

Plus sale summary:
- **Payment method** (CASH, CARD, MOBILE, CREDIT)
- **Cashier name** (who processed the sale)
- **Completion time** (exact date and time)

## 🔍 Common Questions

### Q: Can I expand multiple sales at once?
**A:** No, only one sale can be expanded at a time. When you click a new sale, the previous one automatically collapses. This keeps the view clean and focused.

### Q: Do I need to save or load anything?
**A:** No! The product details are already loaded with the sales list. Clicking just shows/hides them - no extra waiting!

### Q: Can I export the product details?
**A:** The "Export" button exports all sale data including products. When you export to CSV, you'll get product details in the export.

### Q: What if a sale has no items?
**A:** Draft sales (empty carts) will show "0 items" and won't expand because there's nothing to show.

### Q: Can I print the expanded view?
**A:** Yes! The expanded details will print if you use your browser's print function (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P).

## 🆘 Need Help?

### If the details don't show:
1. Make sure the sale has items (not a draft/empty cart)
2. Try refreshing the page
3. Check that you're using a modern browser
4. Contact support if the issue persists

### If you see "0 items":
- This is a draft sale (empty cart)
- Draft sales have no products yet
- Filter by "Completed" to see actual sales
