## 🚀 Major Update: Complete Sales Analytics!

## ✨ NEW Features

### 1. 📊 Sales Summary Dashboard

**See your performance at a glance!**

A beautiful dashboard now appears at the top showing:
- 💰 **Total Sales Volume** - How much revenue you made
- 📈 **Total Profit** - What you actually earned (with margin %)
- 🧾 **Total Tax** - Taxes collected
- 🎁 **Total Discounts** - Money discounted
- 💳 **Payment Breakdown** - Cash, Card, Mobile Money, Credit totals

**No more guessing!** All your key metrics in one place.

---

### 2. 💳 Payment Method Filter

**Filter sales by how customers paid!**

New dropdown lets you filter:
- 💵 Cash sales only
- 💳 Card payments only
- 📱 Mobile Money only
- 🏦 Credit sales only

**Use it for:**
- "Show me all cash sales today"
- "How much did we make from card payments?"
- "Check our credit sales this week"

---

### 3. 💰 Complete Product Financials

**Click any sale to see EVERYTHING about each product!**

Now showing **11 columns** instead of 7:

**NEW COLUMNS:**
- **Cost** - What you paid for the product
- **Tax** - Tax amount + rate (e.g., "$42 (12.5%)")
- **Subtotal** - Final price after tax/discount
- **Profit** - How much you made (Revenue - Cost)
- **Margin %** - Profit percentage with color badge

**Example:**
```
Product: MS Office
Price: $243.56 × 13 qty = $3,166.28
Cost: $150.00 × 13 = $1,950.00
Profit: $1,216.28
Margin: 38.4% ← Excellent! (Green badge)
```

---

### 4. 🎨 Smart Color Coding

**Colors tell you what to do!**

**Margin Badges:**
- 🟢 **Green (≥30%)** → Excellent! Keep this pricing
- 🔵 **Blue (15-29%)** → Good profit margin
- 🟡 **Yellow (<15%)** → Low profit - review pricing
- 🔴 **Red (negative)** → LOSING MONEY - urgent action needed!

**Quick scan** for yellow and red to find problems fast!

## 🎯 Quick Start Guide

### See Today's Profit
1. Filter: Date → "Today"
2. Filter: Status → "Completed"
3. Look at "Total Profit" in summary
4. Done! 🎉

### Find Low-Margin Products
1. Click any sale to expand
2. Look at "Margin %" column
3. Yellow badges = need review
4. Consider price increase

### Filter Cash Sales
1. Click "Payment Method" dropdown
2. Select "💵 Cash"
3. See only cash sales
4. Summary updates automatically

### Check Tax Collected
1. Filter to your date range
2. Look at "Total Tax" in summary
3. Export CSV for records
4. Submit to tax office

## 🚨 Important Notes

### Cost Prices Required!
For profit calculations to work, products need cost prices set in Inventory.

**If you see "N/A":**
1. Go to Inventory Management
2. Find the product
3. Add cost price
4. Save

Future sales will show profit correctly!

### Summary is Per-Page
The dashboard shows totals for **current page only**.

**For complete totals:**
- Use date filters to narrow down
- Increase page size to 100
- Or export to CSV
