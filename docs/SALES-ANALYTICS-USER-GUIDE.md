## 📊 The Sales Summary Dashboard

**Location:** Top of Sales History page (above the sales table)

### What You'll See:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📊 Sales Summary                                    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [ Total Sales ]  [ Total Profit ]  [ Tax ] [ Disc ]│
│   $45,250.00       $18,500.00      $3,200  $1,750  │
│   127 sales        Margin: 40.9%   1,543 items     │
│                                                      │
│ By Payment Method:                                  │
│ 💵 Cash     💳 Card      📱 Mobile    🏦 Credit    │
│   $12,000     $18,000      $10,250      $5,000     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### How to Read It:

| Box | What It Shows | Why It Matters |
|-----|---------------|----------------|
| **Total Sales Volume** | All money received | Your revenue |
| **Total Profit** | Money after costs | What you actually make |
| **Total Tax** | Taxes collected | For tax reporting |
| **Total Discounts** | Money discounted | Impact on profit |

## 🔍 How to View Product Details

### Step 1: Click on Any Sale

Click anywhere on a sale row to expand it.

### Step 2: See the Detailed Table

You'll now see **11 columns** of information:

| Column | What It Shows | Example |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| **Product** | Product name | MS Office Home & Business |
| **SKU** | Product code | SOFT-DL-0002 |
| **Category** | Product category | Software |
| **Qty** | Quantity sold | 13.00 |
| **Unit Price** | Price per unit | $243.56 |
| **Cost** 🆕 | What you paid for it | $150.00 × 13 = $1,950 |
| **Tax** 🆕 | Tax on this item | $42.00 (12.5%) |
| **Discount** | Discount given | -$10.00 (5%) |
| **Subtotal** | Final price | $3,166.28 |
| **Profit** 🆕 | What you made | $1,216.28 |
| **Margin %** 🆕 | Profit percentage | 38.4% |

### Understanding the Colors:

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 🟢 **Green margin badge (≥30%)** | Excellent profit! Keep it up! |
| 🔵 **Blue margin badge (15-29%)** | Good profit - acceptable |
| 🟡 **Yellow margin badge (<15%)** | Low profit - review pricing |
| 🔴 **Red profit** | Losing money - urgent review |

## 💳 How to Filter by Payment Method

### Why Filter by Payment Method?

- See how much cash you collected today
- Track card payment fees
- Monitor mobile money transactions
- Review credit sales (money owed)

### How to Do It:

1. **Find the Payment Filter**
   - Look for the dropdown that says "💳 All Payment Methods"
   - It's next to the Status filter

2. **Select a Payment Method**
   - 💵 Cash - Show only cash sales
   - 💳 Card - Show only card payments
   - 📱 Mobile Money - Show only mobile money
   - 🏦 Credit - Show only credit sales

3. **View Results**
   - Table updates immediately
   - Summary recalculates for that method only
   - See total for that payment method

4. **Clear Filter**
   - Select "💳 All Payment Methods" again
   - Or click "Clear All Filters"

## 💡 Common Questions

### Q: Why does some profit show "N/A"?

**A:** The product doesn't have a cost price set in inventory. To fix this:
1. Go to Inventory Management
2. Find the product
3. Add the cost price
4. Future sales will show profit correctly

### Q: What's a "good" profit margin?

**A:**
- **>30%** = Excellent! You're doing great
- **15-30%** = Good - acceptable margins
- **<15%** = Low - consider increasing prices or reducing costs

### Q: The summary shows different numbers than my total?

**A:** The summary shows totals for the **current page only**. To see totals for all sales:
1. Use date filters to narrow down
2. Or increase page size to 100
3. Or export to CSV for complete totals

### Q: Can I see yesterday's profits?

**A:** Yes!
1. Use the Date Range filter
2. Select "Yesterday"
3. View the summary - it will show yesterday's totals
4. Click on sales to see which products were most profitable

### Q: How do I know how much tax I collected this month?

**A:**
1. Filter status to "Completed"
2. Use date range: "This Month"
3. Look at "Total Tax" in the summary
4. This is what you'll report to tax authorities
5. Export to CSV for detailed records

## 📈 Real-World Examples

### Example 1: Daily Profit Check

**Goal:** See how much profit we made today

**Steps:**
1. Date Range → "Today"
2. Status → "Completed"
3. Look at summary:
   - Total Sales: $1,250
   - Total Profit: $500
   - Margin: 40%
4. **Result:** Made $500 profit today! 👍

---

### Example 2: Cash vs Card

**Goal:** Which payment method customers prefer?

**Steps:**
1. No filters (see all sales)
2. Look at "By Payment Method" in summary:
   - Cash: $12,000
   - Card: $18,000 ← Most popular!
   - Mobile: $10,000
   - Credit: $5,000
3. **Result:** Card is most popular - ensure card machine always works!

---

### Example 3: Find Low-Margin Products

**Goal:** Which products aren't making us much money?

**Steps:**
1. Click on any sale to expand
2. Look at the "Margin %" column
3. Find yellow badges (<15%)
4. Example: "Product X has 8% margin"
5. **Action:** Increase price of Product X or stop selling it

---

### Example 4: Monthly Tax Report

**Goal:** Prepare tax filing

**Steps:**
1. Date Range → "This Month"
2. Status → "Completed"
3. Note "Total Tax": $3,456
4. Click "Export" to get CSV
5. **Result:** Submit $3,456 tax + CSV proof to tax office

## ⚡ Quick Tips

### Tip 1: Check Margins Daily
- Expand top sales each day
- Look for yellow margin badges
- Review pricing for low-margin items

### Tip 2: Monitor Payment Mix
- Check payment method breakdown weekly
- If cash is too high → Promote card/mobile
- If credit is too high → Review credit policies

### Tip 3: Track Discount Impact
- If "Total Discounts" > 10% of revenue → Too generous!
- Review who's giving discounts
- Set discount limits

### Tip 4: Export for Analysis
- Use CSV export for detailed reports
- Import to Excel for charts
- Share with accountant/manager

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Problem: Profit shows "N/A"

**Solution:**
1. Go to Inventory
2. Find the product
3. Add cost price
4. Save

### Problem: Summary seems wrong

**Check:**
- Are you on page 1? (Summary is per page)
- Have you filtered? (Summary is for filtered sales)
- Refresh the page

### Problem: Can't see new columns

**Solution:**
- Scroll right on the table (mobile)
- Or rotate device to landscape
- Or use a larger screen
