## 🎯 Quick Overview

Your POS system uses a **business-centric subscription model**. This means:

- ✅ Each **business** has its own subscription plan
- ✅ One user account can manage **multiple businesses**
- ✅ Each business subscription covers **all storefronts** under that business
- ✅ Different businesses can have different subscription plans

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### How to Access Subscription Management

1. **Click the Subscription Badge** in your dashboard header
   - The badge shows your current subscription status (Active, Trial, Inactive, etc.)
   - Located in the top-right area of your dashboard
   - Click it to open the Subscription Portal

2. **Or navigate directly** to `/app/subscription`

### Your First Time

When you first create your business account:
- ✅ You start with a **Free Plan** (1 storefront allowed)
- ✅ You have **14 days** to explore the system
- ✅ You can upgrade anytime to add more storefronts and features

## 📊 Understanding Your Subscription

### Subscription Badge Colors

The badge in your dashboard header shows your current status:

| Badge Color | Status | What It Means |
|------------|--------|---------------|
| 🟢 **Green** | ACTIVE | Subscription is active and paid |
| 🔵 **Blue** | TRIAL | You're in a trial period (usually 14 days) |
| 🟡 **Yellow** | SUSPENDED | Payment failed, temporary suspension |
| 🔴 **Red** | EXPIRED | Subscription expired, needs renewal |
| ⚪ **Gray** | INACTIVE | No active subscription |

### What Your Subscription Covers

Each business subscription includes:
- **Storefront Limit** - Number of physical locations/shops you can manage
- **Features Access** - Advanced reports, API access, etc.
- **Support Level** - Email support, priority support, or dedicated account manager
- **All Employees** - Unlimited staff members can use the system

## 💼 Managing Multiple Businesses

### If You Own Multiple Businesses

**Example Scenario:**
You own both "DataLogique Systems" (tech company) and "John's Grocery Store" (retail business).

1. **After Login** - You'll see a business selector
2. **Select Business** - Choose which business to manage
3. **Different Subscriptions** - Each business can have a different plan:

1. Click your **profile/business name** in the header
2. Select **"Switch Business"** (if available)
3. Choose the business you want to work with
4. Dashboard updates to show that business's data

**Important:** When you switch businesses:
- ✅ Subscription status changes to the selected business
- ✅ Storefronts list shows only that business's locations
- ✅ Sales data is specific to that business
- ✅ Settings apply to that business only

## 🏪 Storefront Limits

### How Storefront Limits Work

Each subscription plan allows a certain number of storefronts:

| Plan | Storefronts | Price/Month |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Free | 1 | GHS 0 |
| Starter | 3 | GHS 49 |
| Professional | 5 | GHS 99 |
| Premium | 10 | GHS 199 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | Custom |

### What Happens When You Reach the Limit?

**Example:**
You have a Free Plan (1 storefront limit) and already created "Adenta Store".

**When you try to add a 2nd storefront:**
1. ❌ System shows: "Storefront limit reached"
2. 💡 Prompt to upgrade: "Your Free Plan allows 1 storefront. Please upgrade to add more."
3. 🔗 Direct link to view available plans

**After upgrading to Professional Plan (5 storefronts):**
1. ✅ You can now create up to 5 storefronts
2. 📊 Usage tracker shows: "3/5 storefronts used"
3. 🎉 All existing and new storefronts work seamlessly

## 📈 Upgrading Your Plan

### How to Upgrade

1. **Go to Subscription Portal**
   - Click subscription badge in header
   - Or navigate to `/app/subscription`

2. **View Available Plans**
   - See all plan options
   - Compare features and pricing

3. **Select New Plan**
   - Click "Upgrade" on desired plan
   - Review changes

4. **Choose Payment Method**
   - Mobile Money or Card
   - Complete payment

5. **Instant Activation**
   - New limits apply immediately
   - Access to new features instantly

### What Happens When You Upgrade?

**Example:** Upgrading from Free Plan to Professional Plan mid-month

**Before Upgrade:**
- Plan: Free Plan
- Cost: GHS 0/month
- Storefronts: 1/1 used
- Billing date: 1st of each month

**Immediately After Upgrade:**
- Plan: Professional Plan ✅
- Storefronts: 1/5 (can now add 4 more) ✅
- Advanced features unlocked ✅

**Billing:**
- **Prorated charge**: You pay only for the remaining days of the month
- **Example calculation**:
  - Professional Plan: GHS 99/month
  - Upgraded on October 15th (15 days remaining)
  - Prorated charge: GHS 99 × (15/30) = **GHS 49.50**
- **Next billing**: November 1st (full GHS 99)

### Immediate Benefits

After upgrading, you can immediately:
- ✅ Create additional storefronts (up to new limit)
- ✅ Access advanced features
- ✅ Use premium reports
- ✅ Get priority support

## 📉 Downgrading Your Plan

### How to Downgrade

1. **Go to Subscription Portal**
2. **Click "Change Plan"**
3. **Select Lower-Tier Plan**
4. **Confirm Downgrade**

### What Happens When You Downgrade?

**Important:** Downgrades take effect at the **end of your current billing period** (not immediately).

**Example:** Downgrading from Premium to Starter Plan

**Current Situation:**
- Plan: Premium Plan (10 storefronts)
- You have 7 storefronts active
- Billing date: 1st of each month
- Today: October 15th

**You downgrade to Starter Plan (3 storefronts):**

**Before End of Period (Oct 15 - Oct 31):**
- ✅ Still on Premium Plan
- ✅ All 7 storefronts still active
- ✅ Premium features still available
- ⚠️ Warning shown: "Plan will change on Nov 1"

**On November 1st:**
- Plan changes to Starter Plan
- **Problem**: You have 7 storefronts but Starter allows only 3

**System Action:**
- ❌ Blocks creation of new storefronts
- ⚠️ Shows warning: "You have 7 active storefronts. Starter Plan allows 3. Please deactivate 4 storefronts."
- 📋 List of storefronts with "Deactivate" option

**What You Must Do:**
1. Deactivate or delete 4 storefronts
2. Keep only 3 active storefronts
3. Once compliant, system works normally

**Billing:**
- ✅ No refund for unused days
- ✅ Lower price starts on November 1st

## ❌ Cancelling Your Subscription

### How to Cancel

1. **Go to Subscription Portal**
2. **Click "Cancel Subscription"**
3. **Choose cancellation type:**
   - **End of Period**: Keep access until billing date
   - **Immediately**: Cancel right now

4. **Provide reason** (optional but helpful)
5. **Confirm cancellation**

### End of Period Cancellation (Recommended)

**Example:**
- You cancel on October 15th
- Next billing date: November 1st

**What happens:**
- ✅ Access continues until October 31st
- ✅ No charge on November 1st
- ✅ November 1st: Plan changes to Free Plan (1 storefront)

### Immediate Cancellation

**Example:**
- You cancel on October 15th

**What happens:**
- ❌ Access ends immediately
- ❌ No refund for unused days (Oct 16-31)
- ⚠️ Plan changes to Free Plan immediately
- ⚠️ If you have >1 storefront, you must deactivate extras

### After Cancellation

**What happens to your data:**
- ✅ All data is **preserved** (sales, inventory, customers)
- ✅ You can **view** historical data
- ❌ Cannot create new sales (on Free Plan with >1 storefront)
- ❌ Cannot add new storefronts (unless ≤1 active)

**How to reactivate:**
- Easy! Just subscribe to a plan again
- All your data is still there
- Instant reactivation

## 🆘 Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue 1: "Can't Add Storefront - Limit Reached"

**Cause:** You've reached your plan's storefront limit

**Solution:**
1. Go to Subscription Portal
2. Click "Upgrade Plan"
3. Choose plan with higher limit
4. Complete payment
5. Try adding storefront again ✅

---

### Issue 2: "Payment Failed"

**Cause:** Insufficient funds, card declined, or network issue

**Solution:**
1. Check your mobile money/card balance
2. Go to Subscription Portal
3. Click "Retry Payment"
4. Or use different payment method

---

### Issue 3: "Subscription Shows 'Inactive' After Payment"

**Cause:** Payment processing delay

**Solution:**
1. Wait 5 minutes for payment confirmation
2. Refresh the page
3. If still inactive after 10 minutes:
   - Check email for payment confirmation
   - Contact support with payment reference

---

### Issue 4: "Can't Access Subscription Portal"

**Cause:** You're logged in as an employee (not owner/manager)

**Solution:**
- Ask your business owner to manage subscriptions
- Or request subscription management permission

---

### Issue 5: "Downgrade Failed - Too Many Storefronts"

**Cause:** You have more storefronts than the new plan allows

**Solution:**
1. Go to Storefronts page
2. Deactivate or delete extra storefronts
3. Keep only the number allowed by new plan
4. Downgrade will process automatically at period end

### 1. **Keep Payment Method Updated**
- ✅ Update card expiry dates before they expire
