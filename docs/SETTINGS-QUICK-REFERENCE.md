# 🎨 Settings System - Quick Reference

## 📍 Location
**Dashboard → Settings** (left sidebar)

## 📑 Tabs Available

### 1️⃣ Currency & Regional
Configure your business currency and regional preferences.

**Options:**
- **Currency Selector** - Choose from 12 currencies
- **Date Format** - MM/DD/YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY, or YYYY-MM-DD

## 🎯 Quick Actions

### Change Currency:
1. Go to **Currency & Regional** tab
2. Select currency from dropdown
3. Click **Save Changes**

### Switch Theme:
1. Go to **Appearance** tab
2. Click on theme color box
3. Click **Save Changes**

### Enable Dark Mode:
1. Go to **Appearance** tab
2. Click **Dark** button under Color Scheme
3. Click **Save Changes**

### Make Text Bigger:
1. Go to **Appearance** tab
2. Click **Large** under Font Size
3. Click **Save Changes**

---

## 💡 Tips

### For Best Experience:
- **Set currency first** before entering prices
- **Try "Auto" mode** for automatic light/dark switching
- **Use compact mode** on smaller screens
- **Enable high contrast** for long work sessions

### Troubleshooting:
- **Changes not saving?** Click the "Save Changes" button at top right
- **Page looks different?** Check if you're in dark mode
- **Text too small?** Go to Appearance → Font Size → Large
